# Threats

Attack triggers for v50+ on Normal difficulty.

## Sieges and Ambushes

A fortress moves up a tier when it hits **any one** of the tier's thresholds.

| Tier | Population | Created wealth | Exported wealth | What can attack |
|------|-----------:|---------------:|----------------:|-----------------|
| Hamlet | 20 | 5,000☼ | 500☼ | Nothing |
| Village | 50 | 25,000☼ | 2,500☼ | Ambushes only |
| Town | **80** | 100,000☼ | 10,000☼ | Sieges (about 10 soldiers) |
| City | 110 | 200,000☼ | 20,000☼ | Sieges (40 soldiers, 5 monsters) |
| Metropolis | 140 | 300,000☼ | 30,000☼ | Sieges (120 soldiers, 40 monsters, the maximum) |

- Sieges start at a population of 80. The undead are the exception and can attack at any time.
- Once sieges are possible, about 25% of invasions are sieges and the rest are ambushes. Hard difficulty roughly halves the thresholds and raises that share to 50%.
- Goblins can siege you without being formally at war with your civilization. Other civilizations need an actual war.
- Kobolds and cave animal people only ever ambush.

## Forgotten Beasts

- Requirements: **50,000☼ created wealth** and at least one cavern layer you've breached. Population doesn't matter.
- Wealth and noise draw them in. How strongly is set in the difficulty settings.
- Each underground region gets exactly one beast, so worlds with more cavern layers have more of them.

## Megabeasts

Dragons, hydras and rocs need:

- 100,000☼ created wealth
- 10,000☼ exported wealth
- 80 population

Titans have the same requirements but ignore exported wealth. A megabeast with a lair nearby is far more likely to attack than one that lives weeks away.

## Night Creatures

The wiki doesn't give firm triggers for night creatures, werebeasts or vampires. They arrive on the same seasonal schedule as caravans and migrants.

!!! tip "Controlling the pace"
    - Keep population under 80 to hold off sieges and megabeasts.
    - Don't dig into the caverns to hold off forgotten beasts.
    - Heavy trading raises exported wealth, which can push you into a higher tier early.

Sources: DF Wiki pages on [Siege](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Siege), [Forgotten beast](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Forgotten_beast) and [Megabeast](https://dwarffortresswiki.org/index.php/Megabeast).
